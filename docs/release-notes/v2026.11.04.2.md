# v2026.11.04.2 — Long timed tests in Connect keep reaching the live board

**Date:** November 4, 2026

## Fixed

- **Long timed tests stopped reporting.** Each progress report carried the student's whole typed text and prompt, and the server refuses text over 20,000 characters. Once a student passed that, around 40 minutes at 100 WPM, every report was refused: their card froze on the live board, and their final result never reached the host. In own-pace rooms they couldn't move on either. Timed tests now switch to sending only the words around the caret, 50 back and 100 ahead, once the whole text wouldn't fit.
- **Holding a key down** could reach the same limit, in any kind of test, because one long word was never cut. A reported word now stops at 200 characters.
- **Word goals** share this code. After a refresh they now keep accuracy, the ghost and the speed chart for the whole attempt, not just for the words that came back, and the held-down key fix covers them too.

## Changed

- Refreshing during a test this long brings back the student's speed, accuracy, word count, progress, ghost and speed chart from the saved stats. Tests under the limit are unchanged: they still send and restore their whole text.
- Custom text timed tests send every remaining word even when the snapshot is cut down, so a refresh can't end the text early. A host's text is at most 10,000 characters, so ordinary typing never reaches the limit on these tests.

## Known limits

- After a refresh during a very long test, the results screen's correct word and mistake counts and word lists only cover the words typed since a little before the refresh, because the earlier words aren't saved. Speed, accuracy, the word count on the board and the character counts cover the whole attempt.
- Words tests are not part of this fix. A long enough Words test still has a prompt over the limit from its first report, at about 1,300 words on Expert and about 4,300 on Beginner.

## Deploy notes

- No Convex deploy is needed for this fix. `MAX_CONNECT_TEXT_LENGTH` moved to `src/lib/practice-limits.ts` so the browser and server share it, and its value is unchanged. The word goal release this builds on still needs Convex deployed first.
- No data migration. Attempts saved by the old version restore as before.
