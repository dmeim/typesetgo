# v2026.10.27.1 — The UI overhaul

**Date:** October 27, 2026

TypeSetGo now uses date-based versions: the release date plus a counter for more than one release on the same day (`v2026.10.27.1`, `v2026.10.27.2`, …). The version shows in the new footer.

This release brings the whole site onto one new look. Every change also has its own card, with screenshots and working demos, on the new [What's new page](https://typesetgo.app/changelog).

## Added

- **Profile page** (`/profile`). Your stats, history and achievements, plus every setting (theme, text, typing, sound, keyboard), all on one page. Your stats page (`/profile/:id`) is public and can be shared; leaderboard names and the account button link to it.
- **What's new page** (`/changelog`), linked from the bottom of the profile sidebar. It has screenshots and live demos.
- **Keyboard styles.** Spotlight is the new default, plus Finger guide, Keycaps and the classic Legacy keyboard. Choose one on the profile page.
- **Achievement celebration.** When you earn an achievement, a full-screen "Achievement unlocked" plays with the medal, the tier bar filling and the levels around it. Several new achievements play one after another.
- **Site footer.** A thin bar along the bottom with the version, your theme, and links to About, Privacy, Terms and What's new. It hides while you type.

## Changed

- **Dock header.** One pill holds the pages, leaderboard, theme, settings, light/dark, notifications and your account. It hides while you type.
- **Home.** A summary pill for test settings (on phones too) and a stats ticker inside the test. Custom length and your own text now open right under the pill instead of in a dialog.
- **Clean typing screen.** Settings are chosen before a test on the profile page, and nothing about settings appears in the middle of a test. The Theme and Settings buttons open the profile page.
- **Results.** Results fit on one screen, centred with room around them: speed and accuracy compared with your average and best, and a two-axis graph with mistakes marked. Chips open over the graph.
- **Leaderboard.** The Arena layout: Today, 7 days and All time over one podium, with ranks below and your own standing. It opens on Today.
- **Profile settings.** One Preview card shows your theme and practice text together. Live Preview opens your text at actual size in a near-full-screen window. Font cards show just "Aa" and the name, in that font.
- **Kids mode.** It turns on the keyboard with the Finger guide, and puts back your own keyboard setting when you leave.
- **Connect.** A refreshed landing with one Join/Host card, plus a new host panel, live board and student view. Signed-in players join with their account name and picture.
- **Race (ready, not yet open).** The landing and results use the new Clubhouse design; the lobby and race happen on an oval track. The Race button in the Dock stays "coming soon" until races open to everyone.
- **Notifications.** The bell now opens a list grouped by day, where achievements show as collector cards.
- **About, Privacy and Terms.** These pages now have the Dock and tabs to switch between them, and each section starts with a plain-words summary. The legal text itself is unchanged.
- **Fonts.** 45 more typing fonts are now served from TypeSetGo itself.

## Fixed

- On short windows, the styled keyboards now stay on screen.
- Achievement progress now says "1 day to go" instead of "1 days to go".

## Deploy notes

- Deploy Convex before the frontend. Account preferences now include the optional `keyboardStyle` field.
- No data migration is needed.
