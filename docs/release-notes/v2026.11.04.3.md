# v2026.11.04.3 — Long Words tests in Connect work at any length

**Date:** November 4, 2026

## Fixed

- **Long Words tests never reported.** A report carried the test's whole prompt, and the server refuses text over 20,000 characters. A prompt that long was refused from the student's first word, so their card never updated and their result never reached the host. The limit was reached at about 1,300 words on Expert, 1,770 on Hard, 2,360 on Medium, 3,070 on Easy and 4,330 on Beginner, and fewer with punctuation or numbers mixed in. Hosts can set up to 9,999 words. Reports over the limit now carry only the words around the student, as long timed tests do.

## Changed

- Refreshing during a long Words test brings back the words around the student, then deals new words for the rest of the prompt on their next keystroke. The test still ends at exactly the host's word count. Tests under the limit are unchanged: they send their whole text and restore exactly.

## Known limits

- After a refresh during a long Words test, the results screen's correct word and mistake counts and word lists only cover the words typed since a little before the refresh, as with long timed tests. Speed, accuracy and the word count on the board cover the whole attempt.

## Deploy notes

- No Convex deploy is needed. The server's limit and functions are unchanged, so this ships with the Worker alone.
- No data migration. Attempts saved by the old version restore as before.
