# v2026.10.28.1 — New live board cards, and Fit the whole class on screen

**Date:** October 28, 2026

## Added

- **Two card designs on the Connect live board.** The host picks one in the board settings, next to List:
  - **Ribbon** (the default): the student's name is the biggest thing on the card, with their speed beside it, and the whole card fills up as they get through the test. The crown for the fastest player sits next to the name.
  - **Big number**: the speed is the hero, with accuracy beside it, the status and test progress below, and a progress bar along the bottom edge.
  - Long names shrink to fit instead of being cut to a few letters. A popped-out projector board follows the host's pick.
- **Fit on the Connect live board.** One button makes the cards as big as they can be with every student on screen at once, no scrolling. It keeps fitting as students join or leave and when the window changes size. The host tab and a popped-out projector board each fit their own screen. Moving the card-size slider goes back to a size you pick.

## Changed

- **More room for the cards.** The line under the Live board title is gone, and the sort (Joined, Name, WPM, Accuracy, Progress, Your order) sits beside the title, so the cards start higher up the screen.

## Deploy notes

- Frontend only. No Convex changes and no data migration. Board preferences gain `cardStyle` (`ribbon` by default) and `fit` settings, stored in the browser like the rest of the board settings.
