# v2026.11.05.1 — Students' Connect screens no longer overlap

**Date:** November 5, 2026

## Fixed

- **Live stats landed on the text.** While typing, the practice screen holds the top slot at the settings pill's height so the stats can swap in without moving anything. Connect has no pill, so the slot was only 18px tall and the 66px stats spilled over the first line of text. This affected every test type in a room. Connect now reserves the stats' space from the start, so the text also stays still on the first key.
- **The keyboard ran off the bottom of the screen.** The text and keyboard sized themselves against the whole window, but a room's top bar, test indicator and goal bar take up to 180px of it. In a room they now size against the space they actually have.

## Changed

- In a room too short to hold them, the Finger guide hides its hands (below about 684px of practice space). The caption still names the finger to use.
- With the Finger guide in a room, the text gets up to 26% of the practice space rather than 30%, which keeps the hands on screen on more windows.
- Word goals no longer show the sweeping line under the stats, because the goal bar above them shows the real progress.
- A room's test indicator hides its detail line on windows under 704px tall.

## Known limits

- Below about 640px of window height, a word goal room can still need a small scroll to reach the bottom of the keyboard (26px at 600px tall with the Finger guide). The Legacy keyboard needs about 5px at 720px tall. Nothing overlaps.
- On phones, the stats can wrap to a second row once the speed reaches two or three digits, which moves the text down about 18px once.

## Deploy notes

- No Convex deploy is needed. This ships with the Worker alone.
- No data migration. Home and the profile previews keep their existing sizing.
