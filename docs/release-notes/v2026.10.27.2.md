# v2026.10.27.2 — Steady practice screen and site-wide light/dark

**Date:** October 27, 2026

Fixes from the first look at the live UI overhaul. Each change also has a card on the [What's new page](https://typesetgo.app/changelog).

## Fixed

- **Light/dark mode is one choice for the whole site.** Signed in, Home and the Profile page used to re-apply the theme saved on your account every time they opened, and the Dock's light/dark toggle on other pages never reached your account, so each page seemed to keep its own mode. The account theme now applies once when you sign in (a pick you already made that visit wins), and a theme or light/dark pick made on any page is saved to your account.
- **The practice text sits halfway between the settings pill and the keyboard**, or between the pill and the bottom of the screen when the keyboard is off. The shortcut hint no longer pulls it around.
- **The settings pill opens over the text.** The custom length and your-own-text editors no longer push the test down.
- **The on-screen keyboard keeps its place when you click away.** It fades instead of disappearing, so opening the settings pill no longer makes the text jump.

## Deploy notes

- Frontend only. No Convex changes and no data migration.
